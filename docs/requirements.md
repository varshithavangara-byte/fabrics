# Pushpa Fabrics — System Requirements & Specifications

## 1. Executive Summary
Pushpa Fabrics is a modern, responsive, SEO-optimized digital showroom and e-commerce portal built for a luxury Indian textile and fashion brand based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

## 2. Functional Requirements

### 2.1 Navigation & Branding
- Sticky navbar with logo branding, quick call CTA, and mobile drawer menu.
- Smooth scrolling and active page indicator.
- Floating WhatsApp button available across all pages with pre-formatted inquiry message.

### 2.2 Home Page
- **Hero Section**: High-resolution luxury imagery with Framer Motion text animations.
- **About Preview**: Short brand history, 15+ years experience badge, and CTA to full story.
- **Featured Collections**: Grid preview of key product categories (Silks, Lehengas, Fabrics).
- **Services**: Tailoring, custom design, bridal consultation, rentals, and tailoring academy.
- **Why Choose Us**: 6 key value propositions highlighting quality, pricing, and service.
- **Testimonials**: Customer reviews with star ratings.
- **Call To Action**: Quick store visit, call, and WhatsApp action buttons.

### 2.3 Catalog & Categories (9 Categories)
- Sarees, Silk Sarees, Designer Fabrics, Lehengas, Customized Blouses, Wedding Dresses, Couple Dresses, Rental Dresses, Designer Laces.
- Live search bar and tag filtering.
- Direct WhatsApp enquiry button on every card.

### 2.4 Customized Blouse Studio
- Neck pattern showcase with price indicators.
- 7-point measurement guide.
- Custom order submission form.

### 2.5 Wedding & Rental Outfits
- VIP bridal consultation booking.
- Transparent rental pricing, deposit details, and rental terms.

### 2.6 Tailoring Classes
- 3 course levels (Basic, Advanced, Master).
- Duration, fees, and syllabus breakdown.
- Online admission form.

### 2.7 Gallery
- Filterable masonry layout.
- Fullscreen Lightbox image viewer.

### 2.8 Contact & Location
- Contact form sending data to backend REST API.
- Google Maps embed iframe.
- Working hours and address breakdown.

## 3. Technical Requirements
- **Frontend**: React 19, Vite, React Router DOM, Framer Motion, Axios, React Icons.
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB (Mongoose), Nodemailer, Rate Limiter, Helmet, CORS.
- **Design System**: Luxury color palette (Primary: `#8B0000`, Secondary: `#D4AF37`, Background: `#FFF8F3`, Text: `#222222`).
